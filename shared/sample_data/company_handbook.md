# Northwind Analytics - Employee Handbook (2026 Edition)

## 1. Working Hours and Flexibility

Core hours are 11:00 to 17:00 IST. Employees may start any time between 08:00 and 11:00
as long as core hours are covered. A standard week is 40 hours across five days.

Overtime beyond 45 hours in a week must be approved in advance by the reporting manager
and is compensated as time-off-in-lieu within the next 30 days. Northwind does not pay
cash overtime for salaried engineering roles.

## 2. Remote and Hybrid Work

All engineering teams follow a hybrid model: minimum 8 office days per month, with at
least 2 of those days overlapping the team's designated collaboration day.

Fully remote status requires VP approval and is reviewed every 6 months. Employees
working remotely from outside India for more than 30 consecutive days must notify the
People Ops team because of tax residency rules.

Home office stipend: INR 25,000 one-time, claimable after 90 days of employment.

## 3. Expense Policy

| Category | Limit | Approval required above |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Domestic travel (per night hotel) | INR 6,000 | INR 8,000 |
| International travel (per night hotel) | USD 180 | USD 250 |
| Client meals (per person) | INR 2,500 | INR 4,000 |
| Software/tools (annual) | INR 40,000 | INR 40,000 |
| Conference attendance | INR 75,000 | any amount |

Receipts must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Claims submitted after
60 days are rejected automatically by the finance system and require a written exception
from the Finance Director.

Reimbursements are processed in the payroll cycle following approval, typically within
15 business days.

## 4. Equipment and Security

Every engineer receives a company laptop with full-disk encryption enabled. Personal
devices may not be used to access production systems or customer data.

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to security@northwind.example within 4 hours of
discovery. The security team remotely wipes the device and issues a replacement within
3 business days.

Production access requires: completed security training, manager approval, and hardware
2FA. Access is reviewed quarterly and revoked automatically after 45 days of non-use.

## 5. Performance and Promotion

Performance reviews run twice a year, in March and September. Ratings are: Exceptional,
Strong, Meets, Developing, Below.

Promotion decisions happen only in the March cycle. Eligibility requires at least 12
months in the current level and a rating of Strong or above in the two most recent
cycles.

Salary revisions take effect from 1 April and are communicated by 25 March.

## 6. Learning and Development

Each employee has an annual learning budget of INR 60,000 covering courses, books,
certifications and conference tickets. Unused budget does not carry over to the next
financial year.

Certification exam fees are reimbursed at 100% on first attempt and 50% on retakes.
Employees who leave within 6 months of a reimbursed certification repay the amount on
a pro-rata basis.

## 7. Notice Period and Exit

Notice period is 60 days for engineers at L1-L3 and 90 days for L4 and above.
Buyout of notice period is at the company's discretion and costs one month of gross
salary per 30 days waived.

Final settlement, including unused leave encashment, is released within 45 days of the
last working day.
