NORTHWIND ANALYTICS - LEAVE POLICY (effective 1 January 2026)

1. ANNUAL LEAVE (EARNED LEAVE)

Every full-time employee earns 24 days of annual leave per calendar year, credited at
2 days per completed month of service. Employees who join mid-year receive a pro-rated
credit.

A maximum of 12 unused annual leave days may be carried forward into the next calendar
year. Anything above 12 days lapses on 31 December. Carried-forward days must be used
by 30 June of the following year or they lapse.

Annual leave of 3 or more consecutive days must be applied for at least 7 calendar days
in advance. Leave of 5 or more consecutive days requires a documented handover note
approved by the reporting manager.

2. SICK LEAVE

12 days of sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not carry forward and is not
encashable.

A medical certificate is required for sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days. For
absences longer than 10 consecutive days, People Ops may request a fitness-to-work
certificate before the employee returns.

3. CASUAL LEAVE

6 days per calendar year, intended for short, unplanned personal matters. Casual leave
cannot be clubbed with annual leave for more than 3 consecutive days, and cannot be
taken in half-day units more than twice a month.

4. PARENTAL LEAVE

Maternity leave: 26 weeks of paid leave for the first two children, 12 weeks thereafter,
as per the Maternity Benefit Act. An additional 8 weeks of unpaid leave may be requested.

Paternity leave: 15 working days of paid leave, to be taken within 6 months of the
child's birth or adoption. It may be split into a maximum of 3 blocks.

Adoption leave: 12 weeks for the primary caregiver, for a child below 3 years of age.

5. BEREAVEMENT LEAVE

5 days for the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling) and
2 days for extended family. No documentation is required.

6. LEAVE WITHOUT PAY

Leave without pay is granted only after all applicable paid leave balances are exhausted
and requires approval from both the reporting manager and the department head. Periods
of leave without pay exceeding 30 days in a year reset the employee's annual leave
accrual for the remaining months.

7. ENCASHMENT

Only annual leave is encashable, and only at the time of exit. Encashment is calculated
on basic salary, not gross salary, and is capped at 30 days.

8. HOLIDAY CALENDAR

10 fixed public holidays plus 2 floating holidays that the employee chooses from a
published list. Floating holidays must be used within the calendar year.

9. APPROVAL AND ESCALATION

Leave requests are approved or rejected within 3 business days. If a manager does not
respond within 5 business days, the request auto-escalates to the department head.
Emergency leave may be applied retroactively within 48 hours of returning to work.
